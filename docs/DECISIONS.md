# Decisions

Every judgment call is recorded with its reason and how to undo it. This file holds the defaults the template ships
with: the choices the first build made on a real ten-year export, stated without anyone's data. Your own build's
decisions go in `data/DECISIONS.md`, next to your data and outside git (CLAUDE.md, ground rule 9).

## Defaults

| Area | Decision | Reason | How to undo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input | `make unzip` uses `data/export.zip` when present, else the newest `data/*.zip` that contains `apple_health_export/export.xml`, and keeps a copy in `data/archive/export_<ExportDate>.zip`. `export_cda.xml` (clinical documents) is never unzipped. | Keep the user's own file name; the clinical file can be larger than everything else together and is not used. | Remove the `-x` exclusion in the Makefile. |
| Time | Every timestamp carries the phone's UTC offset at export time, even for winter dates and trips. UTC instants are correct; local time comes from a time-zone timeline: workout `HKTimeZone`, else `timezonefinder` on the route start, else the home zone. A trip's zone covers its workouts and two travel days either side; the home zone fills the rest (between two away zones the switch is halfway). Fixed-offset zones such as `Etc/GMT+4` (Peloton) map to the named zone with the same offset at that instant. | Using the offset would put winter walks an hour late; fixed-offset zones fragment the timeline. Switching halfway between a home workout in June and a trip in September put six weeks at home on Mountain time. | `clean.assign_time_zones`, `geo.tz_segments`. |
| Parse | Workout metadata written twice is deduplicated by key; one workout's several GPX files are numbered continuously in time order; GPX points with `vAcc < 0` have no elevation; devices are classified from the `device` string, never the source name. | Export quirks (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3). | `parse.py`. |
| Years | The app's first year is the first year with an outdoor on-foot GPS workout; daily data before it stays in the tables. | Route chapters need routes; daily charts can still show earlier years. | `export.year_min`. |
| HRmax | 99.5th percentile of workout heart rate in the two years ending at the last run, ride or interval session (not simply the last two years). Workouts whose heart rate came from an older watch's files get their own maximum by the same rule over that era. | Two years of only walks measure walking peaks, not maximum; a younger heart scored against today's maximum inflates zone 5. | `heart_rate.max_bpm` in config.yaml; `derive.file_era_hr_max`. |
| GPS | G3 door jitter trims points that stay within 25 m of the start or end for more than 45 s. G4 cuts sustained (≥ 60 s) stretches above 12 mph at either end and excises them in the middle; isolated jumps under 30 s become their own gap type (dashed, no distance). The watch's distance stays the headline unless it implies more than 5 mph. G6 scaling is skipped when GPS covers less than half the workout. | Drives happen at the start and middle too; the watch usually did not count the drive; a 10-second track scaled to the watch's distance made a 6-second mile. | `track.TrackParams`. |
| Elevation | A Hampel filter before Savitzky–Golay; climb counted with a 2 m hysteresis state machine that counts every metre to the peak. Headline climb: the watch's barometer, else USGS 3DEP, else smoothed GPS. | Altitude glitches of hundreds of metres; the naive hysteresis drops the last rise before every summit. | `track.smooth_elevation`, `climb_hysteresis`. |
| Workouts | W1 merges copies (≥ 80% overlap in the same family, or the same distance and duration with a clock shifted by whole hours), keeping the copy with a route, then heart rate, then the watch, then the native source. W2 marks walks and runs without GPS as `no_route`. W5: a final pause never resumed ends the workout. | Apps sync the same session; a sync while travelling can shift the clock; a watch left running after a pause made a 12-hour "walk". | `clean.rule_w1`, `rule_w2`, W5 block. |
| Steps | Per minute: watch, then phone, then other; within a class the larger source wins, never the sum. A day with no samples is null. | Naive sums overstated steps by about 70% on days both were carried. | `clean.rule_s1`, `dedupe.source_priority`. |
| Heart rate | Never interpolated across gaps (5 s grid, forward-filled up to 30 s). TRIMP uses Banister's average-HR form for every workout; the 5 s sum is kept as `trimp_stream` where coverage is 80%+. On-foot workouts averaging under rest + 15 bpm have their HR withdrawn, not zeroed. | Older exports hold only a few samples per walk; a loose band reads as "most efficient walk". | `derive.workout_hr_metrics`. |
| Pace | Grade-adjusted pace: Tobler's hiking function for walks and hikes, Minetti's running polynomial for runs; grades from the watch's barometric altitude where available, smoothed over ~90 m. The steepest-stretch record must hold in both the DEM and the barometer. Best efforts count distance only while moving. | Minetti's walking curve is metabolic and made descents look twice as slow on flat ground; DEM elevation beside cliffs is unreliable. | `metrics.gap_speed`, `derive.track_metrics`. |
| Loops | One-cell-buffered overlap on a 25 m grid (0.85) and seeded clustering; leaderboards on walks only. | Plain Jaccard fails on a 2 m shift; connected components chain neighbourhood routes into one loop. | `metrics.buffered_overlap`, `derive.find_loops`. |
| Exploration | New ground = distance × the share of a route's 30 m cells never visited before. Street coverage counts streets (20 m, sidewalks count for their street) and paths & trails (15 m) separately, without driveways or parking aisles; a segment is walked at 60% coverage. | Cell counts overstate winding tracks; driveways and sidewalks distort the denominator. | `derive.new_ground`, `osm.py`. |
| Weather | `auto`: the nearest NOAA GHCNh station within 30 km for temperature, humidity, wind, precipitation and rain/snow/storm; Open-Meteo for the rest and for cloud cover. Requests use 0.25° cells, yearly batches, 4 s apart. Duration-weighted means skip missing hours. | Closest to Apple's own temperatures in the first build (1.8 °F mean difference); reanalysis over-reports drizzle; ceilometers stop at 12,000 ft. | `weather.source` in config.yaml. |
| Elevation data | USGS 3DEP 1/3″ tiles (fixed 0.1° tiles, never centred on home) in the US; Open-Meteo elevation elsewhere. | Free, no key, one consistent source. | `enrich.py`. |
| Streets | OpenStreetMap from a Geofabrik regional extract, filtered locally to a box around home (0.05° snapped). | Offline after one download; Overpass throttles per IP. | `exploration.osm_source: overpass`. |
| Strava | Optional second source. Only `activities.csv` and `activities/` are unzipped. Strava workouts use Strava's distance (`app:strava`); the device's odometer is kept as metadata; file heart rate belongs to its workout only. | Each workout matches the app it would be checked against. | Remove the Strava zip. |
| Privacy | Home is the densest cluster of route starts and stays in the database. Share mode hides 200 m at both ends of home routes and a 300 m zone whose centre is offset 100–150 m from home; the app receives per-point flags, never coordinates. | End trims alone left loops passing the house visible. | `privacy` in config.yaml. |
| App | hyparquet instead of DuckDB-WASM; GPU filtering on the atlas; panels redraw only near the viewport; maps stay on the night ground in both themes; copy states what the data shows, never an assumed direction. | First paint and filter budgets; real data may say the opposite of a success story. | `app/src`. |
| 3D view | Height is the date, the time of day or the ground's elevation; the floor is one place, clipped to a square around 98% of its points; elevation is exaggerated to fill about 60% of the height and the factor is printed. Colour follows the height (year, or start time). | A third axis shows what the flat atlas cannot (when, not only where); clipping keeps one long route from flattening the rest; an unlabelled exaggeration would mislead. | `app/src/map/stack.ts`. |
| Video | The tour keeps time at 128 BPM: sections start on bar lines, clicks land on beats, and the page reports its timeline so the soundtrack (synthesised by `stride.soundtrack`, nothing to license) is laid under it. Share mode is on unless `--no-share`. Both a silent and a music copy are written. | Cuts on the beat read as intentional; platform music libraries can replace the bed. | `scripts/record.mjs`, `app/src/present.tsx`. |
| Demo site | The public demo is `make demo` then `make site`, built and deployed by GitHub Actions to Pages, never from anyone's export; it carries a banner and demo wording (`VITE_STRIDE_DEMO=1`) and link-preview tags. It deploys only where the repository variable `DEMO_SITE` is `true`. | People decide in seconds whether to clone; a fork should not inherit failing deploys. | `.github/workflows/pages.yml`. |
| Demo generator | A random stream per workout, day, town and schedule, keyed by what it is; values from `exp`, `sin` and `arctan2` rounded before writing (elevation to the millimetre, speed to 0.1 mm/s, course to 0.01°). CI compares the generated export's content digest with `demo/digest.json`. | One shared stream turned last-bit differences between Apple Silicon and x86 Linux into a different demo (different routes and counts) from the same seed. | `stride.demo.Demo._rng`; `make demo-digest` after an intended change. |
| Sleep | A separate Sleep page. Nights run 18:00 to 18:00 local and are named by the evening; one source per night (stages, then asleep, then in bed; the watch first; never a sum); naps are daytime sessions of 10 min to 4 h; sources that record no time awake are estimates. No correlation with the activity data. | Exports mix several sleep eras and sources for the same nights; a Garmin window read as sleep adds about two hours a night. | `pipeline/stride/sleep.py`; remove `sleep` from `make all`. |
| Milestones | Life milestones live in `data/milestones.yaml` (never committed), edited by hand or in the app (`make dev` writes the file through `stride.milestones`; a static build keeps edits in the browser). Drawn faintly on every date axis; share mode hides labels unless `public`. | Context the data cannot know (a child born, working from home) explains most of its turns; family dates must not leak into screenshots. | `app/src/milestones.ts`. |
| Determinism | Run stamps use the export's `ExportDate`; Parquet is ordered, written single-threaded, doubles rounded to 6 decimals. `make verify-clean` must report identical outputs. | Ground rule 6. | `export.copy_parquet`. |
| Breathing | Breathing gets its own chapter on the Sleep page: nightly breathing disturbances, the 30-day windows sleep apnea notifications judge, and the simplest rule that separates an export's notified windows from its quiet ones (a nightly level and a share of nights above it, fitted on a 0.5-per-hour grid from 5 to 20 and reported with how many windows it matches). With no notified or no quiet window there is no rule. | Apple publishes no threshold, and a fixed number would present a guess as fact; a rule fitted to the user's own notifications is checkable, and is labelled a reconstruction, not a diagnosis. | `sleep.breathing`; the `breathing` chapter in `components/sleep.tsx`. |
| Privacy | Share mode leaves the Breathing chapter out entirely (and its place in the chapter nav); the 15-second video tour never goes near it. | Sleep apnea screening is medical information; screenshots and videos are made in share mode. | `SHARE_HIDDEN` in `components/sleep.tsx`. |
| Heart and movement | Cardio recovery matched to the workout that ended within 10 minutes of each sample, weekly walking steadiness dated by the week's last day, daily median stair speed. The six-minute walk estimate is counted but not drawn when every value is the same number. | Real exports can carry years of six-minute walk values that are all exactly 500 m: a flat line would look like a measurement. | `export.export_body`, `charts/body.ts`. |
| Units | `ft/s` joins the unit table (to m/s). | Stair speed arrived in ft/s and was stored unconverted, against "SI inside". | `units.CONVERT`. |
| Sound | Environmental readings are summarised by medians (each reading is already an average over about 30 minutes, and the median shrugs off a single loud half hour); headphone level is the energy average over listening time, because exposure adds up in energy, not in decibels. Day is 7:00 to 22:00, night 22:00 to 7:00 named by its evening. | Typical levels for the grid and the lines; the physically right average where the number is about exposure. | `export.export_sound`. |
| Performance | Nothing redraws while the page is still: the 3D view turns only while a third of it is on screen, at most 30 frames a second, for a minute at a time, and stops for good (and drops to one device pixel) when ten frames in a row arrive more than 120 ms late; pick layers draw only in the picking pass (`layerFilter`); the 3D build yields every 150 routes; no backdrop blur over WebGL canvases. `scripts/perf.mjs` checks it. | A strong Windows laptop crawled on the public demo: the off-screen 3D view redrew on every refresh (about 480 WebGL draw calls a second at rest), and Chrome on a hybrid-GPU laptop draws on the integrated GPU, often at 144 Hz or more. | `Stack` tick in `map/stack.ts`; the `layerFilter` props. |
| Video | A 15-second cut of the tour (`?present=short`, `record.mjs --short`): eight bars at 128 BPM, the routes under the title, then one beat per new feature, then the end card; the music drops as the 3D view lands. | For posting a release update, where a 70-second tour is too long. | `shortSteps` in `present.tsx`. |
